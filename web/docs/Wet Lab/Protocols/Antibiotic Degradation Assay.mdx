# <a id="3fcd65dd82be80849346fcf0f6e058d8" />Purpose

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80148e31f22001def067" />This protocol outlines a method to compare the antibioitc degradation capacities of different bacterial samples using our CpxP and RecA biosensor strains. To determine antibioitc degradation kinetics, this assay may be performed at multiple antibiotic exposure durations.

# <a id="3fcd65dd82be8082878ce9f34ae0a600" />Materials

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80298dc2fc553f5925f6" />Glycerol stocks of antibiotic-degrading bacterial strains
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be802daa67c9a0c1b69ee1" />Glycerol stocks of biosensor strains
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be804e82e9dda12df21674" />LB-Cm broth (17 mg/L)
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be804aaf29db23dcc18761" />Antibiotic stock solutions
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8005b395cf08673a5b6b" />0.2 µm sterile filter and syringe
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b2b9caced4bf7ce0fc" />Multichannel pipette & reservoir
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be808382b5dd3cb374be6c" />96-well microplate (flat-bottom, sterile)
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80f9b208e6b8e1ef84ef" />Microplate reader

# <a id="3fcd65dd82be80179876c892d274ee0e" />Method

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be80cbb9dfc47f342c78b5" />Day 1: Preparation of cultures

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8076be16e7053a5e24c9" />Using glycerol stocks of each biosensor and antibiotic-degrading strain, inoculate 10 mL of LB-Cm media.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be804b9e50e6447ece352f" />Incubate at 37 ºC overnight, shaking at 220 rpm.

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be803881c4e5d2746325b9" />Day 2: Antibiotic degradation period

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b782dceea94504e925" />Measure OD600 of each antibiotic-degrading bacterial culture.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80e2a3abd0fcd4f171ac" />Dilute the cultures to an OD600 range of 0.1-0.4 using LB-Cm.
3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8063aa78fad154ba3b8e" />Take 2 mL of the liquid culture. Introduce antibiotic stock to predetermined concentration(s)
   - <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b5897fea3aa6cb495f" />e.g., the MIC for our RecA and CpxP biosensors ranges from 4-8 µg/ml for gentamicin, tobramycin, and amoxicillin
4. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8063a7fddc36def1df47" />Incubate at 37˚C, shaking at 220 rpm for 2-12 hours.
   - <a id="3fcd65dd82be8088a538eb7e9632b793" />To determine degradation kinetics, test a range of antibiotic exposure durations in separate tubes

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be809d9157e2c63b011f2d" />Day 2: Assay Plating

### <a id="3fcd65dd82be80278251eb3ec7d41fde" />**Harvesting antibioitc-containing supernatant**

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be801284c5f5edbade44d6" />Spin down the antibiotic-treated cultures at 4000 rpm for 10 minutes.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80858f0fe2d7f9189d16" />Transfer the supernatant into fresh tubes. Be careful not to disturb the cell pellet, leaving behind 100 µl of supernatant if necessary.
3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be806e9d4fd77face9d87c" />To remove excess bacteria, filter each supernatant through a sterile 0.2 µm filter.

### <a id="3fcd65dd82be8017a67bc0dd2f9be4b8" />**Preparing biosensor cultures**

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80d98d69ee4e699f4b36" />Measure OD600 of each biosensor strain.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8049b20bea3ec42f0b15" />Dilute the biosensor cultures to an OD600 of 0.1 using LB-Cm.

### <a id="3fcd65dd82be802d87c0d1f64bdc664f" />**Plating the assay**

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80a7a1a8cc272798b221" />To a 96-well plate, combine 100 µL of biosensor culture and 100 µL of antibiotic-containing supernatant per well, for a total of 200 µL per well.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8044b29bd0a9b46239ac" />Include the following controls:
   1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be809f8be4d20d4fa0dcfa" />**Biosensor growth control:** 100 µL biosensor culture and 100 µL of LB-Cm
   2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80e09828ecea708eede8" />**Blank supernatant control**: 100 µL LB-Cm and 100 µL antibiotic-containing supernatant
   3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be800cac66ffabe3db653b" />**Blank LB-Cm control**: 200 µL LB-Cm
3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80cbbf98d95d20f46ce6" />Incubate at 37˚C for 18-24 hours.

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be80ca8e27dd0ff95fefa8" />Day 3: Readout

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8088b0b9e4b04121aed7" />Measure fluorescence during the first 4-8 hours of incubation.
   - <a id="3fcd65dd82be801fbdb7d74b03523dc3" />Excitation: 488 nm
   - <a id="3fcd65dd82be80bc9f1efb002cbd6d83" />Emission: 510 nm
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80cbb1c2fd8506966e9e" />Measure OD600 after 18-24 hours of incubation.

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be805ca661ebb438f9afbb" />Data Analysis

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80e080a3dbfbe1d2c661" />Use the OD600 values of the blank controls to rule-out contamination.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be805781ffd6a796fc40e1" />Calculate ΔOD by normalizing OD600 values to the biosensor growth control wells, which display the baseline growth of the biosensor strains. These have been not been treated with antibiotic-containing supernatants.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b98f20fa824c463699" />Calculate fluorescence intensity fold-change relative to the biosensor growth control wells, which display the baseline fluorescence of the biosensor strains under no antibiotic stimulation.

## <a id="3fcd65dd82be800b985beac5c0d8472c" />Data Interpretation

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8037a107f3fb6bcfb30a" />ΔOD that is near 0 indicates that significant antibiotic degradation has occurred (i.e. growth is not inhibited such that the final OD600 of treated wells is similar to that of the biosensor growth control).
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b2b740f78594a25f7a" />ΔOD that is slightly \< 0 indicates that partial antibiotic degradation has occured (i.e. growth is only slightly inhibited such that the final OD600 of treated wells is slightly lower than that of the the biosensor growth control)
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80889126e35bccba6f44" />ΔOD that is significantly \< 0 indicates that partial antibiotic degradation has occured (i.e. growth is inhibited such that the final OD600 of treated wells is much lower than that of the the biosensor growth control)
