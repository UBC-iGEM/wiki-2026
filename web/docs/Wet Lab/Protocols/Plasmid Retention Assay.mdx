# <a id="3fcd65dd82be8060afd0c53db5587900" />Purpose

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8008abbfcfb605c668b9" />To determine the maximum amount of time for which a transformed strain (e.g., RecA  or CpxP biosensor strain) retains its plasmid when cultured in the absence of antibioitc selection.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8063b17ce97965047a61" />This protocol outlines a method to quantify plasmid retention time by culture bacteria with or without antibiotics, then comparing their growth on selective agar plates. The appearance of colonies will indicate plasmid retention, relative to a positive control (transformed strain grown in selective media) and a negative control (WT strain).

# <a id="3fcd65dd82be807fadb7dbd9065a6e72" />Materials

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8044ad5fde876d9cc2d0" />Glycerol stock of WT strain
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80829f48d71fc5457f1f" />Glycerol stock of transformed strain(s) with Cm selection marker
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80128a95c27d5ac79fc1" />LB-Cm (17 mg/L)
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8034b88bf1397f1c6dce" />LB
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be801b960ec82aca280e5b" />LB or LB-cm agar plates
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be802a8b2cfcb13a30ea77" />Cuvettes
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8032bf71dac7014c7eb2" />Eppendorf tubes

# <a id="3fcd65dd82be8052ba31d2af5c8c3932" />Method

### <a id="3fcd65dd82be80ecaa16d661eb57e799" />Day 1-2: Preparation of Strains

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80018e11c91a4ec89fd5" />From glycerol stock, streak each strain onto LB agar plates (+/- Cm). Incubate overnight at 37˚C.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8085a083e7dfc9c678e1" />Inoculate an isolated colony of each strain into 10 mL of LB-Cm, or LB for the WT strain. Incubate at 37˚C overnight, shaking at 220 rpm.

### <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b6b463eaf7ebfbe5ee" />Day 3: Plating the assay

1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80f090dcd223c58ae3aa" />Measure the initial OD600 of the cultures.
2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80feb401dad7ed7db29e" />Split each culture into 2 tubes, 5 mL each.
3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80cf8486f50470f82014" />For one tube, dilute the culture to an OD600 of 0.1 in LB-Cm.
4. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80658b8cd25ba45017b8" />For the other tube, spin down the culture at 4000 xg for 10 minutes. Resuspend in 5 mL of Cm-free LB and dilute to an OD600 of 0.1 in LB.
5. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80d8bd1ef86ba8020ff1" />Incubate all tubes at 37˚C, shaking at 220 rpm. Take the culture out of the incubator at different timepoints (0hr, 1hr, 2hr, 4hr, 8hr, 16hr, 24hr).
6. <a id="3fcd65dd82be804290bad08e0bc0c21c" />For each culture:
   1. <a id="3fcd65dd82be805dace4e31d8a9021b7" />Vortex the tube
   2. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80a0b8bfc8b663d6ca90" />Record the OD600 of the culture
   3. <a id="3fcd65dd82be80e4b7f7c6564898e21d" />Dilute the solution to an OD of 0.1
   4. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8055b2a6f02e52fb3659" />Mix 100 uL of the culture with 900 uL of LB or LB-Cm media. Perform a 6-step 1:10 serial dilution to achieve a 10^-7 dilution. Repeat one more 1:10 dilution for 10^-8 and 10^-9.
   5. <a id="3fcd65dd82be8078a70cd5aa529cdf98" />Divide the LB-Cm agar plate into 3 sectors, labelling them -7 to -9. To each, add three 2 uL drops of the corresponding dilution to the plate.
   6. <a id="3fcd65dd82be801ba9e8fcac853e764e" />Allow the spots the fully absorb, then invert and incubate at 37˚C overnight.

# <a id="3fcd65dd82be80d19208e92cd8841f0a" />Data Interpretation

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80c4b3eeff89448d9002" />**Negative control**: For the WT strains cultured in either LB or LB-Cm, no growth of colonies on LB-Cm agar plates is expected.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be809db38edc68ab40122c" />**Positive control**: For the transformed strains cultured in LB-Cm, the growth of colonies on LB-Cm agar plates is expected.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be802d8da8d50aae8e33c3" />For transformed strains cultured in the absence of antibiotic selection (LB):
  - <a id="3fcd65dd82be80dbaff5c5ea70d9473f" />**Retention**: the growth of colonies on LB-Cm agar plates indicates that the plasmid is retained at this time point.
  - <a id="3fcd65dd82be80da84c0f515b4e9a013" />**No Retention**: the absence of colonies on LB-Cm agar plates indicates that the plasmid is not retained at this time point.
