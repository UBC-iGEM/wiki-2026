# <a id="3c5d65dd82be8086bef8f97f0f86f6ed" />Background

## <a id="3c5d65dd82be80c583bfdb93af47b3f9" />Objective

<a id="3c5d65dd82be8023a9a7c8cc8b840522" />Primary: Quantify plasmid retention over time in our biosensors when held without Cm selection at room temperature

<Callout icon="🗣">
  <a id="3c5d65dd82be80cc86b4f3ac4f3bf416" />For what duration of time can be keep the biosensors in media without antibiotics at room temperature?
</Callout>

## <a id="3c5d65dd82be80e88092c13a439fed3f" />Variables

<a id="3c5d65dd82be80549562d7380898690c" />Independent: Time

<a id="3c5d65dd82be8047b22cdaec322af144" />Dependent: Plasmid Retention Fraction ($CFU_{LBCM}/CFU_{LB}$)

## <a id="3c5d65dd82be8035ba72d44d970578bd" />Procedure

### <a id="3c5d65dd82be80e8883fd3b94d5eb098" />Day 0 & 1 - Strain Prep

1. <a id="3c5d65dd82be800a96f2d48241538150" />From glycerol stock, streak a place per strain, leave overnight to grow.
2. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80ee8655d34b12b618b1" />Inoculate 3 colonies of each strain into separate 3mL LB-Cm (LB for Control strain) and grow overnight (37C, 200rpm)

### <a id="3c5d65dd82be8074acb8c19ade2fc97d" />Day 2 & 3 - Experimentation

1. <a id="3c5d65dd82be808b822fd37c75975a0a" />Measure starting OD600, diluting to a linear range if needed
2. <a id="3c5d65dd82be804cb4e9d551df7ca7b2" />Pellet \~1mL and wash once in Cm-free LB
3. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80ef8128df4bb7ae8e66" />Inoculate each strain to a starting OD600 of 0.05, then transfer 5mL to a culture tube. You should have 6 tube types.

1. <a id="3c5d65dd82be8078a874f5999890ec08" />Culture tubes at 37C @ 200rpm
2. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80c69ab4fa582edf11e4" />For each timepoint (0m, 1 hr, 2 hr, 4 hr, 8 hr, 24 hr) per tube
   1. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80dea96bda9515acd326" />Vortex the tube and aspirate
   2. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80918d4fda4ca59ad8aa" />Record the OD600 of the solution
   3. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80359353c1f62e69d939" />Dilute the solution in the cuvette to an OD of 0.1. If the recorded OD is lower than 0.1 for the earlier timepoints, skip this step.
   4. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80e896e2edd3970ba5c1" />Mix 100uL of the solution in the cuvette with 900uL of LB-Cm media. Perform a 6 step 1:10 serial dilution. (-1, -2, -3, -4, -5, -6)
   5. <a id="3c5d65dd82be80979e33d8ec71389171" />Divide a plate into 4 sectors, labelling them -3 to -6. To each, add 3 10uL spots of the corresponding dilution to the plate.
   6. <a id="3c5d65dd82be801da79fe0aa71270fe6" />Allow the spots the fully absorb, then invert and store in 37C overnight

## <a id="3cad65dd82be803ca0d0c2d4ecbc9cd6" />Summary

- <a id="3cad65dd82be806cae85cfd35129b85b" />As expected, the Epi300 cultures did not survive on LB-Cm plates.
- <a id="3cad65dd82be80c28d8df8b90f377f56" />This data shows that CpxP and RecA strains retain their reporter plasmid when cultured in Cm-free LB media for at least 1 hour.
- <a id="3cbd65dd82be80938895ea4ca127466c" />Experiment should be repeated with higher dilutions to prevent lawn of growth
