# <a id="3e4d65dd82be8095b492cbf390e5144c" />Background

<a id="3e4d65dd82be8042a473cb95aecee04c" />We will expose 6 ug/ml of each antibiotic (gentamicin, kanamycin, or amoxicillin) to their appropriate patient simulant strain for either 6 or 12 hours. The concentration was selected based on the results of [Biosensor Challenge Assay](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/biosensor-challenge-assay), which identified a MIC range of 4—8 ug/ml for the biosensor strain. This sets the maximum threshold of undegraded antibiotic that can be exposed to our biosensor, thereby setting the maximum limit of antibiotic input that can be exposed to our patient simulant strains.

## <a id="3e4d65dd82be801aac10f3f74daefc97" />Objective

<Callout icon="🗣">
  <a id="3e4d65dd82be80a9b295c9d807a94ea1" />**To what degree do our patient simulant strains degrade their corresponding antibiotics after 6 or 12 hours of exposure?**
</Callout>

<br id="3e4d65dd82be80d08e39cd3b895f367c" />

# <a id="3e4d65dd82be801bbc5ad38b4c9fa0e4" />Experiment:

## <a id="3e4d65dd82be8080bd87e7eb707eb76c" />Procedure:

## <a id="3e4d65dd82be80ed8bf2de615bde9e33" />Day 1 - Preparation of Patient Simulant Cultures

1. <a id="3e4d65dd82be80b288f9c6f7971fbfe1" />Using glycerol stocks of each strain, inoculate 8 mL of LB-Cm media.
2. <a id="3e4d65dd82be80fc9464fa4367a18700" />Incubate at 37 ºC, shaking at 250 rpm (starting \~9 pm)
   - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80b9ba06fe31be63559f" />GmR simulant
   - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80d793c5f7f3acfe5593" />KanR simulant
   - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80108010e3054bb9ae0a" />Beta-lactamase simulant

## <a id="3e4d65dd82be80adb4cad07367ef1c1b" />Day 2 - Antibiotic degradation using patient simulant cultures

<a id="3e4d65dd82be80359470f7d8fd144f98" />We will expose our antibiotics to degradation conditions, incubating for 2 different durations:

- <a id="3e3d65dd82be808a96f5d66966888df3" />12-hour incubation (treat at 8am)
- <a id="3e3d65dd82be80f2b692ee871b10db60" />6-hour incubation (treat at 2pm)

<br id="3e4d65dd82be80d9a6ebd5f3579e9ecf" />

<a id="3e4d65dd82be806081fffdfc94ea2c8b" />**12-hour antibiotic degradation period**

- <a id="3e4d65dd82be80df9cf0d9aaf1993242" />Read OD of each culture (\~12hr incubation)
  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80f3a7b8cd412a3c4cdf" />Blank with LB-Cm
  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80a4b3d8e7bf0f93987e" />Record the OD for each strain
- <a id="3e4d65dd82be80ea8660caee8aeffd5f" />Take 2ml of culture. Treat with 6 ug/ml of each antibioitc
  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be808889d7db0348a4a7c3" />GmR culture + gentamicin (e.g. 120ul of 100ug/ml stock into 1.88ml of culture)
  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be802db69bd51d5ff886c3" />KanR culture + kanamycin (e.g. 120ul of 100ug/ml stock into 1.88ml of culture)
  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be803492fcc718ac0c0c75" />Beta-lactamase culture + amoxicillin (e.g. 120ul of 100ug/ml stock into 1.88ml of culture)
- <a id="3e4d65dd82be800fbecbe29fb4c873c8" />Incubate at 37˚C, shaking at 250 rpm for \~12 hours (until 8:30pm)

  <a id="3e4d65dd82be80babe39f28671f1b3e7" />KanR: 0.582

  <a id="3e4d65dd82be804a992dc8665a4d60bd" />We do not have kanamycin, used tobramycin for KanR

  - <a id="3e4d65dd82be80ad86c7d7cb38fdd4d3" />GmR: 0.63 (1.42ml culture, 580ul LB-Cm)

## <a id="3e3d65dd82be8026944cebdf1aa96672" />Day 3 - Readout

- <a id="3e3d65dd82be80698cc1f4b4585e85ad" />OD remains low (near 0.1) = no antibiotic degradation after 6 and/or 12 hrs
- <a id="3e3d65dd82be80048cdbd325bf2e7394" />OD slightly increases = some antibiotic degradation after 6 and/or 12 hrs
- <a id="3e3d65dd82be80bb8435e09cc8881c3a" />OD increases and/or increases similarly to unstimulated control = antibiotic degradation occurs after 6 and/or 12 hrs
- <a id="3e3d65dd82be8083bf20fe600a2a5043" />Measure fluorescence at:
  - <a id="3e3d65dd82be80eb82bfee0f3a020911" />Ex: 488 nm

## <a id="3e3d65dd82be802ba5bfe4ef076818d4" />Results

<a id="3e6d65dd82be806d90c2c706d9ea2dbd" />The plates were incubated for 24 hours.

<a id="3edd65dd82be80eab076ecb8b9c311f5" />We observed that the OD values of the simulant supernatant control wells increased, suggesting that bacteria from the pellet remained in the supernatant. As such, the raw data was not analyze. However, trends are described below:

- <a id="3e6d65dd82be80e7acb2c21d8f303f20" />For both biosensor strains, the OD values of wells treated with tobramycin-containing and amoxicillin-containing supernatants remain similar to the untreated controls. This demonstrated that bacterial growth was not inhibited by antibiotics, thereby indicating that the KanR and beta-lactamase patient simulant strains facilitate antibioitc degradation. However, the fluorescence intensity measurement is lower than the untreated controls, indicating that the degraded antibioitc product may impact our biosensor’s ability to fluoresce.
- <a id="3e6d65dd82be80ac8b4fdb2387b00f79" />For both biosensor strains, the OD values of wells treated with gentamicin-containing supernatant decreases by 2-fold relative to untreated controls. The inhibited growth indicates that initial amount of gentamicin was only partly degraded by the GmR patient simulant strain.
  - <a id="3e6d65dd82be809e9abddb215e0f87c7" />This aligns with the observations made in [Simulant Survival Titration v1](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/simulant-survival-titration-v1), demonstrating the partial degradation of gentamicin between 4-8 ug/ml. The patient simulant itself may have been inhibited at 6 ug/ml, causing a decreased capacity for gentamicin degradation.

## <a id="3e6d65dd82be80c78551e3beeadbef2c" />Summary

<a id="3ebd65dd82be80bc9190e9fba8885dc2" />This experiment is confounded by the growth of residual bacteria from the pellet which persisted in the supernatant. As such, tt is necessary to perform a second attempt of this experiment. Next time, we will use a 0.2uM filter to remove any bacteria from the supernatant.
