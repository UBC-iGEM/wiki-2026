# <a id="3add65dd82be8046a671c2965025681c" />Purpose

<a id="3add65dd82be80ecb891da5f90c64c3b" />Following the preliminary GFP expression sensitivity assay in [Biosensor GFP sensitivity assay v1 ](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/biosensor-gfp-sensitivity-assay-v1-), we will perform a subsequent titration to narrow down the antibiotic concentration range for optimal GFP expression.

<a id="3add65dd82be8085af2becd41087d991" />For the RecA reporter, we will use a narrowed down MIC range based on the findings described in Takeaway 4.1  from[Biosensor GFP sensitivity assay v1 ](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/biosensor-gfp-sensitivity-assay-v1-). For the CpxP reporter, we will investigate GFP expression induced by antibiotic concentrations that are higher than literature MIC values, based on the finding that the stimulated Cpxp biosensor is not significantly distinguishable from the negative control (refer to Takeaway 4.2 in [Biosensor GFP sensitivity assay v1 ](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/biosensor-gfp-sensitivity-assay-v1-)).

<a id="3add65dd82be801ca1e1f9ad1428b4e3" />Additionally, we will test the impact of higher bacterial densities on GFP expression, aiming for an $OD_{600}$ values of 0.4 and 0.8.

<a id="3add65dd82be80998332f9a5a35db030" />In the end, we hope to identify an optimized antibiotic concentration range and incubation time range for each antibiotic that allows for a differential fluorescence signal relative to the unstimulated control.

<a id="3add65dd82be808fb942d6b8eb2566ce" /><u>Day 2: GFP stimulation assay</u>

- <a id="3add65dd82be80c8826dc17678654aa9" />Measure the $OD_{600}$ of each overnight culture in a cuvette, using LB-Cm as the blank.
- <a id="3add65dd82be800ab800f15ecea1572d" />Create at least 4 ml of bacterial culture diluted to half the $OD_{600}$ of the original culture. Reserve the remaining culture.
  - <a id="3add65dd82be808f9529dac14372784d" />1X culture: original culture
  - <a id="3add65dd82be8043bb40d061fd64f16e" />0.5X culture: diluted culture
- <a id="3add65dd82be802c8e58fe8c419e31db" />For two 96-well plates, add 100 µl of condition media in triplicate according to the layout in Figures 1 and 2.
  - <a id="3add65dd82be80f8af5bf00f280a7926" />Use a 96-well deep well plate to allow the transfer of media with a multi-channel pipetting into Rows A—G.
- <a id="3add65dd82be80a995d2f25cbbd2cc63" />For Rows G—H on each plate, make the control wells:
  - <a id="3add65dd82be809291e6ff7093dc8a5c" />Blank controls (12 wells/plate): do not add cells to row G; add an additional 100 µl of media
  - <a id="3add65dd82be80bd9860fced0c7a94cc" />Blank LB-Cm control (2 wells/plate): 200 µl of LB-Cm media
  - <a id="3add65dd82be8070b5cbc71be3854ca6" />Unstimulated biosensor (3 wells/plate): 100 µl of culture and 100 µl LB-Cm
- <a id="3add65dd82be80ca948ffc54c00345ac" />For each biosensor strain, add 100 µl of bacterial suspensions into the appropriate wells, according to the layout in Figures 1 and 2.
- <a id="3add65dd82be80edb836c692f7a068f6" />Incubate the plate for 30 at 37˚C, using an excitation wavelength of 488 nm and measuring fluorescence at 510 nm.
  - <a id="3afd65dd82be80bbb86ed3e91c701082" />Measure every 2 minutes for the first 10min interval
  - <a id="3afd65dd82be8005bdb2f7f93694e068" />Measure once every 10 mins

# <a id="3add65dd82be801e9726f5a085596e98" />Summary

<a id="3b4d65dd82be806d87a5f3ef10febb76" /><u>CpxP reporter titration</u>

- <a id="3b4d65dd82be8060b0c2fc276070fd43" />For most conditions, there is a peak in fluorescence output at t = 0 mins, which rapidly decreases after 10 minutes. However, sfGFP is known to have a maturation of nearly 10 minutes. This could suggest the presence of confounding factors at time of plating, which impacts fluorescent readout.
- <a id="3b4d65dd82be80d78bccc89862355c2e" />The results demonstrate a slight improvements in CpxP reporter response for gentamicin at 8 µg/ml, although a broader range of concentrations should be tested to determine maximal fluorescence output.

<br id="3b4d65dd82be80048cc0fbf2e442e2dc" />

<a id="3add65dd82be80779952e72c6bb9a843" /><u>RecA reporter titration</u>

- <a id="3b4d65dd82be80e89f1ac229cd888730" />Cephalexin at 3 µg/ml provides a stable GFP expression stimulus throughout 30 minutes.
- <a id="3b4d65dd82be80498958e04a46419c7f" />Amoxicillin at 6 µg/ml GFP expression peaks at 20 minutes.
- <a id="3b4d65dd82be802883e1ebb3aeaacc39" />For the concentration conditions carried over from [Biosensor GFP sensitivity assay v1 ](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/biosensor-gfp-sensitivity-assay-v1-), the resulting fluorescence intensity readouts had limited similarities. Inter-experimental variation suggests that additional experimental variables may have influenced fluorescence measurements, limiting reproducibility across trials.
