# <a id="3a0d65dd82be8068a5d6d8ae48e38a25" />Purpose

<a id="3a0d65dd82be80b89d5ae33f790cec80" />To generate our biosensor strain, we will transform our two reporters (*recA* and *cpxP*) into non-pathogenic K-12 derivative *E. coli*, EPI300.

# <a id="3a0d65dd82be80e59e54ddf6e425ef3f" />Materials & Methods

<a id="3a0d65dd82be80b99ad1fcc2df413579" /><u>Materials</u>

- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80b1aaaad6f74b1150d2" />Chloramphenicol LB agar plate (17 mg/L)
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80c89c79d8a99bb1b42f" />EPI300 (stocks provided by Hallam Lab)
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be804ea54ae753f0c69abc" />Cloned reporters (RecA and CpxP constructs) from [Miniprep for CpxP and RecA reporter plasmids](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/miniprep-for-cpxp-and-reca-reporter-plasmids)
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80d4bdffc0f9cc7771fc" />NEB SOC Outgrowth Medium
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be801991abc5fb52843f9f" />42°C water bath

<br id="3a0d65dd82be80708081cf16d6fc0136" />

<a id="3a0d65dd82be80e19e9aec82f56a2309" /><u>Methods</u>

1. <a id="3a0d65dd82be80f7b7e2f2c2756147c9" />Mix 45 µL of EPI300 stock with 5 µL of cloned plasmid (*recA* or CpxP)
   1. <a id="3a0d65dd82be8059b7a2c2d9095ad34d" />pipette up and down to mix
2. <a id="3a0d65dd82be8050baf8e983f729ed19" />Incubate on ice for 30 minutes
3. <a id="3a0d65dd82be80a191c9eff3857d64cc" />Heat shock at 42°C for 30 seconds
4. <a id="3a0d65dd82be80838b73fda79cca5def" />Incubate on ice for 5 minutes
5. <a id="3a0d65dd82be80e2b1c1e5c627fb20e9" />Add 100 µL of SOC outgrowth medium and incubate at 37°C for 1 hour
6. <a id="3a0d65dd82be8006b9fbe8fe99288b56" />Plate 50 µL on each plate and incubate at 37°C overnight

# <a id="3a0d65dd82be806180c2f5503319e7c3" />Summary

<a id="3a0d65dd82be809ba412f2c583018d6f" />Colony growth on selective plates demonstrates the successful transformation of CmR-containing plasmids into EPI300. These biosensor strains will be saved in glycerol stocks and used for subsequent validation experiments.
