# <a id="3ead65dd82be8049b24ff63f824bca3c" />Background

<a id="3ead65dd82be80fcbe3ef76e904609e4" />In the previous experiment ([Simulant Degradation Assay v1](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/simulant-degradation-assay-v1)), we found that our supernatants contained residual bacteria from the pellets. Thus, this was a confounding variable in our analysis, requiring us to repeat this experiment with the additional of a filtration step before plating the assay.

<a id="3ead65dd82be805fa7a9f3b93ad0feb4" />For this experiment, we will test this at 8µg/ml since it is the upper limit of the biosensor MIC range, and 16µg/ml since it is 2-fold the upper limit of the biosensor MIC range.

## <a id="3ead65dd82be804a9df3e0ba9ad625c9" />Objective

<Callout icon="🗣">
  <a id="3ead65dd82be80b4a4f0f4a16eb4ee93" />**To what degree do our patient simulant strains degrade their corresponding antibiotics after 6 or 12 hours of exposure?**
</Callout>

# <a id="3ead65dd82be804992c4c6d4457eeef5" />Experiment:

## <a id="3ead65dd82be8033acebcfc76397d2e1" />Materials:

- <a id="3ead65dd82be80c29864c8076271d588" />Standard 96-well microplate (flat-bottom, sterile)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80889a62cde8f61a6187" />Multichannel pipette + reservoir
- <a id="3ead65dd82be803c94f6c791fe740c23" />0.2 µm sterile filter and syringe
- <a id="3ead65dd82be806e9ad4d26d8ba7caf3" />Gentamicin (Sigma Aldrich, #G1264-50MG)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be8069b3adfcd413162c91" />Kanamycin (BioShop, #KAN201)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80e6bcc5f7e5ba7b0ace" />Amoxicillin (Sigma Aldrich, #A8523-1G)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80a3b975cbf906ee0842" />MilliQ water
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80b99faaf37fa2e62cab" />2 biosensor strains
- <a id="3ead65dd82be8095be90d839404d92b2" />3 Patient simulant strains (from [Patient Simulant (KanR, GmR) Cloning](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/patient-simulant-kanr-gmr-cloning) and [Patient Simulant (Beta-Lact., CmR backbone) Cloning](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook/patient-simulant-beta-lact-cmr-backbone-cloning) )
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80efb768fffb4a359938" />LB-Cm broth (17 mg/L)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80068111ffa85a1cb010" />Varioskan plate reader

## <a id="3ead65dd82be806093eef72eb27cbf55" />Procedure:

## <a id="3ead65dd82be8000a84cfc61f6210576" />Day 1 - Preparation of Patient Simulant Cultures

1. <a id="3ead65dd82be80a2bb26d250cd7875f3" />Using glycerol stocks of each strain, inoculate 10 mL of LB-Cm media.
2. <a id="3ead65dd82be80e196dff096e14ebf35" />Incubate at 37 ºC, shaking at 250 rpm (starting \~9 pm)
   - <a id="3ead65dd82be80518c17c16bb05a2767" />GmR simulant
   - <a id="3ead65dd82be807fab8acdae01e01d71" />KanR simulant
   - <a id="3ead65dd82be80378395eb71082bb0e8" />β-lactamase simulant
   - <a id="3ead65dd82be8043a25ccadb3b26e082" />RecA biosensor
   - <a id="3ead65dd82be800ca364d65e1bb87779" />CpxP biosensor

## <a id="3ead65dd82be808b8238fca169929ed9" />Day 2 - Antibiotic degradation using patient simulant cultures

<a id="3ead65dd82be80afa6c5dbf9bfd0bfce" />We will expose our antibiotics to degradation conditions, incubating for 2 different durations:

- <a id="3ead65dd82be80398136df897d058a88" />12-hour incubation (treat at 11am)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be8049b6f6cab2bc55d60c" />6-hour incubation (treat at 3pm)

<br id="3ead65dd82be80fbbc98dd1458e53655" />

<a id="3ead65dd82be80408f2ddc62e27bcfee" />**10-hour antibiotic degradation period**

- <a id="3ead65dd82be80f5a5a0d5afe07eb67a" />Read $OD_{600}$ of each culture (\~20hr incubation)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80e19d16e2c907baff10" />Blank with LB-Cm
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80cd965ec4335024332f" />Record the $OD_{600}$ for each strain
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80b8ba22f1e3c5b47aad" />Take 2ml of culture. Treat with 8 µg/ml or 16 µg/ml of each antibiotic. You will have a total of 6 tubes.
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80b88807d0c8d1fd60ad" />GmR culture + gentamicin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml culture)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80c8b97bf3e040815049" />KanR culture + tobramycin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8086b361c51112e614bb" />β-lactamase culture + amoxicillin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80b89c0adaa35daab708" />Incubate at 37˚C, shaking at 250 rpm for \~12 hours (until 8:30pm)

<a id="3ead65dd82be80ef9b98f9a66475d082" />**6-hour antibiotic degradation period**

  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8051a170dd9ea731c1b5" />Blank with LB-Cm
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be802596fbe070ba75ba8b" />Record the OD for each strain

  - <a id="3ebd65dd82be80f9abe3ffed5a4e2e9b" />GmR culture + gentamicin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml culture)
  - <a id="3ebd65dd82be8044b4b0c58186aa1506" />KanR culture + tobramycin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture)
  - <a id="3ebd65dd82be800296d1ff561b441e3e" />β-lactamase culture + amoxicillin (e.g. 160 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.84ml of culture; 320 µl of 100µg/ml stock + 1.68ml of culture)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80f6a8f6f2f9d52e1be5" />Incubate at 37˚C, shaking at 250 rpm for \~6 hours

## <a id="3ead65dd82be80a68a67f459ff225fdf" />Day 2 - Assay Plating

<a id="3ead65dd82be80f99d5bc202c112a786" />**Harvest antibioitc-containing supernatant**

- <a id="3ead65dd82be809ab67be88a8f4244ca" />Spin down at 4000 rpm for 10 mins
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80ab8455ce1f46ceab6a" />Collect supernatant into a fresh tube. Be careful not to disturb the cell pellet, leaving behind 100 µl of supertant if necessary. There should be \~1.5ml for each of 12 tubes.
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be804bb1b6c3516bc1f60c" />12-hour gentamicin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80af81c3dd8082e5e36a" />12-hour kanamycin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8026b9e9e32f27e7c0c4" />12-hour amoxicillin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8046b8e7c009465efb84" />6-hour gentamicin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80cb8f14fae41346a59a" />6-hour kanamycin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80d5a8f0d48617e4953a" />6-hour amoxicillin supernatant, x 2 concentrations (8 or 16 µg/ml)
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80a7be31e9deaca072a6" />Filter each supernatant through a sterile 0.2 µM filter.

<a id="3ead65dd82be80ce8d79c48f467028f1" />**Prepare biosensor cultures**

- <a id="3ead65dd82be80678c42d068047af911" />Measure $OD_{600}$ of each biosensor strain
- <a id="3ead65dd82be806fa5c0f6baddd64445" />Dilute biosensor cultures to an $OD_{600}$ of 0.1 using LB-Cm

<a id="3ead65dd82be80e78693c6a2547ef3a8" />**Plate the assay**

- <a id="3ead65dd82be80a2b790ebef773ca8cd" />Plate a total of 200 µl/well according to the plate layout below.  You will have 2 plates total, 1 plate per concentration of antibioitc.
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8068b858cae92eb182a4" />**Biosensor antibiotic treatment groups** (2 biosensor strains x 3 treatments x 2 timepoints x 3 wells = 36 wells)
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be8061bca9d9c8bb7ddd8b" />100 µl OD-corrected biosensor culture
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be80e28525e1920f3aa814" />100 µl antibiotic supernatant
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80a3b92be62a5e967d59" />**Biosensor no-antibiotic control groups** (2 strains x 3 wells = 6 wells)
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be806084cee4a9616da358" />100 µl OD-corrected biosensor culture
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be8010ab5eeb1f24c9d833" />100 µl LB-Cm
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be8082a1fbcc469ee94ec6" />**Media controls**
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be80a6aebac826a4ca01f7" />LB-Cm blank: 200 µl LB-Cm
    - <a id="3ead65dd82be800fac55c1f2ed2ea94b" />supernatant blank: 100 µl LB-Cm + 100 µl supernatant
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be803ab09fe7a268af674b" />Incubate at 37˚C for 24 hours.

## <a id="3ead65dd82be80bd9df4d983a5e0cd7e" />Day 3 - Readout

<a id="3ead65dd82be805fb63fd8d01dcd86a4" />**Measure** $OD_{600}$ **and fluorescence after 24 hrs of incubation**

- <a id="3edd65dd82be80e7835afa4bf21fe87b" />Measure OD at 600 nm.
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80f78413d8cb57f0a904" />OD remains low (near 0.1) = no antibiotic degradation after 6 and/or 12 hrs
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be801b9083c28782eadc34" />OD slightly increases = some antibiotic degradation after 6 and/or 12 hrs
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80029921c4666f3710a9" />OD increases and/or increases similarly to unstimulated control = antibiotic degradation occurs after 6 and/or 12 hrs
- <a id="3ead65dd82be80c99093f72d4ea0e87d" />Measure fluorescence at the following settings:
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be80a2bb5fca17a29d7289" />Excitation: 488 nm
  - <a id="3ead65dd82be808f8dc3ce08f1f81f33" />Emission: 510 nm

## <a id="3edd65dd82be80ecb9f0f97cf6700463" />Summary

<a id="3eed65dd82be80e9836cdbc77454e111" />This experiment tested the capacity of our patient simulant strains to degrade antibiotics. Our aim was to determine the degree of antibioitc degradation by exposing our biosensors to supernatants harvested from the following cultures:

- <a id="3eed65dd82be80e19f39d7861b903bb1" />Patient simulant (initial OD of 0.1) exposed to MIC limit (8 µg/ml) for either 6hr or 10hr
- <a id="3eed65dd82be80c6a1bac9f8bb9f4a7d" />Patient simulant (initial OD of 0.1) exposed to 2-fold MIC limit (16 µg/ml) for 6hr or 10hr
- <a id="3eed65dd82be80e298dffddef737e963" />Patient simulant (initial OD of 0.5) exposed to MIC limit (8 µg/ml) or 2-fold MIC limit (16 µg/ml) for 6hr
