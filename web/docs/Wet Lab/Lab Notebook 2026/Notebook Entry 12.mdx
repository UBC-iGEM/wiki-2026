## Procedure

<span underline="true">Day 1: Prepare bacteria and antibiotic solutions</span>

- For each biosensor strain, inoculate bacteria into 50 ml of LB-Cm media (17 mg/ml). Incubate at 37˚C and 250 rpm overnight (> 16 hours).
- For WT, incubate at 37C and 250 rpm agitation in 50mL LB only media.

1. Reconstitute antibiotics in MiliQ water and sterilize through 0.2uM filter.
   - Gentamicin (4 mg/ml)
   - Kanamycin (20 mg/ml)
   - Amoxicillin (1 mg/ml)
   - Cephalexin (2 mg/ml)
   - See reconstitution protocol from [mention-page](https://app.notion.com/p/38ad65dd82be80b98f23c20278bee82e)
   - This is the **Master Stock**
2. Prepare Working Stocks from **Master Stocks** based on appropriate dilution above
3. Measure the OD600 of each overnight RecA and CpxP culture
4. Preparation of 2X Antibiotic Tubes:
   - For each tube add the antibiotic working stock volume and then add LB-Cm to a final total volume of 2.00 mL according to the 8 tables above.
   - Vortex each antibiotic condition thoroughly
     - Antibiotic solutions are prepared at 2X the desired final concentrations because they will be mixed 1:1 with bacterial culture

<span underline="true">Day 2: Prepare bacteria and antibiotic solutions</span>

1. Plating:
   - For each experimental well:
     1. Add 100µl of the appropriate 2X antibiotic solution.
     2. Add 100µl of the appropriate culture 2X OD600
        1. Final well volume 200**µl**
        2. Final concentration = 1X
   - For unstimulated controls:
     - 100 µl bacterial culture
     - 100 µl antibiotic-free medium
   - For blanks:
     - media only, no cell
   - Water moat:
     - Place DI H2O in the recesses between wells.=<br>
2. Robot Measurement
   - Following preparation, load the plate into the biofactorial/plate-reader workflow
   - Incubate the plate at 37C
   - Record:
     - OD600
     - GFP Fluorescence
     - Normalized GFP to OD600
   - Record measurements every 10 minutes for 2 hours
     - Timepoints: 0, 10, 20, 30, … 100, 110, 120(13 measurements per condition)
