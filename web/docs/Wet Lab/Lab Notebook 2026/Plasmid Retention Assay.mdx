# Background

## Objective

Primary: Quantify plasmid retention over time in our biosensors when held without Cm selection at room temperature <Callout icon="🗣" color="green_bg">
For what duration of time can be keep the biosensors in media without antibiotics at room temperature? </Callout>

## Variables

Independent: Time
Dependent: Plasmid Retention Fraction ($CFU_{LBCM}/CFU_{LB}$)
Dependent: sfGFP Florescence normalized to OD600

# Experiment

## Materials and Reagents

- LB-Cm (17 mg/L)
- LB
- LB Plates
- Cuvettes
- Dilution tubes

## Strains

| Name | Purpose          |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| RecA | Directly testing |
| CpxP | Directly testing |
| WT   | Control          |

## Procedure

### Day 0 & 1 - Strain Prep

1. From glycerol stock, streak a place per strain, leave overnight to grow.
2. Inoculate 3 colonies of each strain into separate 3mL LB-Cm (LB for Control strain) and grow overnight (37C, 200rpm)

### Day 2 & 3 - Experimentation

1. Measure starting OD600, diluting to a linear range if needed
2. Pellet \~1mL and wash once in Cm-free LB
3. Inoculate each strain to a starting OD600 of 0.05, then transfer 5mL to a culture tube. You should have 6 tube types.

|             | RecA | CpxP | Control (EPI300) |
| ----------- | ---- | ---- | ---------------- |
| LB Media    | RLX  | CLX  | ELX              |
| LB-Cm Media | RLC  | CLC  | ELC              |

1. Culture tubes at RT @ 200rpm (do we even have shakers at RT?)
2. For each timepoint (0m, 1 hr, 2 hr, 4 hr, 8 hr, 24 hr) per tube
   1. Vortex the tube and aspirate
   2. Record the OD600 of the solution
   3. Dilute the solution in the cuvette to an OD of 0.1. If the recorded OD is lower than 0.1 for the earlier timepoints, skip this step.
   4. Mix 100uL of the solution in the cuvette with 900uL of LB-Cm media. Perform a 6 step 1:10 serial dilution. (-1, -2, -3, -4, -5, -6)
   5. Divide a plate into 4 sectors, labelling them -3 to -6. To each, add 3 10uL spots of the corresponding dilution to the plate.
   6. Allow the spots the fully absorb, then invert and store in 37C overnight

## Summary

- As expected, the Epi300 cultures did not survive on LB-Cm plates.
- This data shows that CpxP and RecA strains retain their reporter plasmid when cultured in Cm-free LB media for at least 1 hour.
- Experiment should be repeated with higher dilutions to prevent lawn of growth
