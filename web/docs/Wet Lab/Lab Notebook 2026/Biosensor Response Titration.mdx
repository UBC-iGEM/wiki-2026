# Background

## Objective

<Callout icon="🗣" color="green_bg">
	What is the response of the two biosensor strains to differing concentrations of antibiotics below its MIC?
</Callout>

From [Biosensor Challenge Assay](/wet-lab/lab-notebook-2026/biosensor-challenge-assay), we determine that the MIC of the two biosensor strains, CpxP and RecA lies between 4 µg/mL to 8 µg/mL for Tobramycin, Amoxicillin, and Gentamicin. In this experiment, we will aim to establish a dose-response curve between fluorescence and sub-inhibitory antibiotic concentrations. We will be using a set OD of 0.1 per well as the challenge assay MIC was determined with this OD.

# Experiment

## Materials and Reagents

- Gentamicin (Sigma Aldrich, #G1264-50MG)
- Tobramycin
- Amoxicillin (Sigma Aldrich, #A8523-1G)
- Cephalexin (AmBeed, #A320894)
- MilliQ Water
- 0.2 uM sterile filter
- LB-Cm media (17 mg/ml)
- RecA and CpxP biosensor strains (from [Transformation of CpxP/RecA into Epi300](/wet-lab/lab-notebook-2026/transformation-of-cpxp/reca-into-epi300))
- 96-well plates, flat bottom
- Varioskan microplate reader

# Procedure

## Day 1 - Culture Inoculations

Inoculate two culture tubes of approximately \~7mL of LB-Cm media with RecA and CpxP respectively. Leave overnight at 37C, shaking @ 200rpm.

## Day 2 - Plating and Readout

### Antibiotic Reconstitution

1. Reconstitute all three antibiotics to 100ug/mL, correcting for potency.
   1. Amoxicillin: 900 µg / mg
   2. Gentamicin: 600 µg / mg
   3. Tobramycin: 972 µg / mg
2. Sterile filter all antibiotics

### Working Stock Preparation

1. Into seperate Eppendorf tubes, mix 80 µL of the 100 µg/mL each antibiotic stock with 920 µL of LB-Cm media to make 1 mL of working stock.

   ### Plate Preparation

The following plate layouts will be used

***

1. For each moat well, dispense 200uL sterile water
2. Dispense 100uL LB-Cm into all interior wells except column 2 (cols 3-11, rows B-G)
3. Dispense an additional 100uL LB-Cm to the sterility well (G11)
4. Dispense 200uL of the working stock into column 2
5. Mix throughly (4-5 up-downs), then pipette 100uL into the next column to the right
6. Repeat step 4 until you reach column 10, discard 100uL from column 10
   1. You should have 100uL in each well, with 2x the concentration of antibiotic
7. Repeat steps 4-6 for all rows
8. When you are done with a plate, remember to place the lid back on

### Inoculum Preparation

1. Obtain the overnight culture and mix throughly
2. In a 1cm cuvette, 900 uL of LB and read OD 600
   1. Use plain LB media as a blank
3. Adjust the suspension with LB to a target OD600 of 0.2
4. Invert the tube to mix

### Plate Addition

1. Except for the media blank and moat wells, add 100uL of the prepared inoculum to all interior (non-moat) wells

### Reads

1. Read the OD and fluorescence of the plates immediately. After reading, return the plates to a 37C incubator.
2. Repeat this reading at 30min, 1hr, 2hrs, 4hrs and 8hrs
   1. Started 16:54
   2. Next readings: 17:24, 17:54, 18:54, 20:54, 01:02
