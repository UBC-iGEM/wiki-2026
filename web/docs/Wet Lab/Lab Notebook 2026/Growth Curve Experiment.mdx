# Background

## Objective

To measure the baseline growth kinetics of each strain and determine burden of the backbone w/wo promotor+sfGFP. <Callout icon="🗣" color="green_bg">
**How do our biosensors grow compared to normal EPI300, and EPI300+CmR, at both 37C and RT (22C)?** </Callout>

Another piece of information we obtain is the level of basal expression of GFP at RT and 37C.

## Variables

Independent: Bacterial Strains

| ID | Strain                 | Role                       |
| -- | ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1  | EPI300, no plasmid     | Growth Ceiling             |
| 2  | EPI300, empty backbone | Measuring cost of backbone |
| 3  | EPI300, RecA           | Biosensor strain           |
| 4  | EPI300, CpxP           | Biosensor strain           |

Dependent: OD600 + fluorescence measurement at each timepoint

# Experiment

## Materials and Equipment

- LB Broth
- LB-Cm Broth (17mg/ml)
- 96-well flat bottom, clear plate + lid
- DI water
- Culture tubes

## Procedure

### Day 1+2 - Streak and Starter

1. Streak each strain and grow overnight at 37ºC
   1. Use LB agar plates for strain 1, LB-Cm plates for strains 2-4
2. Pick 3 colonies per strain, inoculate into separate tubes w/ 3mL of media each and grow overnight at 37C , 200rpm
   1. Use LB for strain 1, LB-Cm for strains 2-4

|                          |               |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| Condition                | Tube IDs      |
| Wild Type (Strain 1)     | WT1, WT2, WT3 |
| Backbone-only (Strain 2) | BB1, BB2, BB3 |
| RecA (Strain 3)          | RC1, RC2, RC3 |
| CpxP (Strain 4)          | CX1, CX2, CX3 |

### Day 3 - Plating + Experiment

1. Measure OD600 of each tube, diluting into linear range if needed
2. For cells cultured in LB-Cm, pellet \~1mL, discard supernatant and resuspend in LB media, LB-Cm for those with plasmids.
3. Dilute all cultures to 0.02 OD600 in LB media
4. Dispense 200uL per plate using layout below. Create a total of 2 plates
