## Procedure

<span underline="true">Day 1: Prepare bacteria and antibiotic solutions</span>

- For each biosensor strain, inoculate bacteria into 50 ml of LB-Cm media (17 mg/ml). Incubate at 37˚C and 250 rpm overnight (> 16 hours).
- For WT, incubate at 37C and 250 rpm agitation in 50mL LB only media.

1. Reconstitute antibiotics in MiliQ water and sterilize through 0.2uM filter.
   - Gentamicin (4 mg/ml)
   - Kanamycin (20 mg/ml)
   - Amoxicillin (1 mg/ml)
   - Cephalexin (2 mg/ml)
   - See reconstitution protocol from [mention-page](https://app.notion.com/p/38ad65dd82be80b98f23c20278bee82e)
   - This is the **Master Stock**
2. Prepare Working Stocks from **Master Stocks** based on appropriate dilution above
3. Measure the OD600 of each overnight RecA and CpxP culture, make sure it reaches **OD600 = 0.8**
4. Preparation of 2-fold Serial Dilutions
   1. <span discussion-urls="discussion\://3c4d65dd-82be-80d6-a203-f71741c4cfcc/3c4d65dd-82be-805a-97c0-c23770bab096/3c4d65dd-82be-8013-bd4e-001cebac576d">Label 14 tubes for each antibiotic (1-14, Antibiotic Initial)</span>
   2. Add 700 ul LB-Cm to tubes 2-14
   3. Add 1400 ul of the highest 2X antibiotic working stock to tube 1
   4. Transfer 700 ul from tube 1 to tube 2
   5. Mix tube 2 thoroughly (vortex)
   6. Transfer 700 ul from tube 2 to into tube 3
   7. Repeat sequentially through tube 13
   8. Do not transfer antibiotic into tube 14, it will be the antibiotic-free LB-Cm as the 0 ug/ml control
   - Each tube will contain 700 ul of antibiotic solution, 600ul from each tube will be used for the six experimental wells (3 for each reporter), leaving 400ul excess
   - Vortex each antibiotic condition thoroughly before dispersing into wells
     - Antibiotic solutions are prepared at 2X the desired final concentrations because they will be mixed 1:1 with bacterial culture

<span underline="true">Day 2: Prepare bacteria and antibiotic solutions</span>

1. Plating:
   - For each experimental well:
     1. Add 100µl of the appropriate 2X antibiotic solution according to the plate layout in the Google Sheet.
     2. Add 100µl of the appropriate culture 2X OD600 (= 0.8 —> 0.4)
        1. Final well volume 200**µl**
        2. Final concentration = 1X
   - For unstimulated controls:
     - 100 µl bacterial culture
     - 100 µl antibiotic-free medium
   - For blanks:
     - media only, no cell
   - Water moat:
     - Place DI H2O in the recesses between wells.<br/>
2. Varioskan measurement

- Immediately following plate preparation, begin the fluorescence assay using the Varioskan microplate reader
- Incubate the plate at 37C during the assay
- Record GFP fluorescence measurement over 40mins every 10 minutes
  - Ex: 488 nm
  - Em: 510 nm
  - Record measurement over 2 hours every 10 minutes
