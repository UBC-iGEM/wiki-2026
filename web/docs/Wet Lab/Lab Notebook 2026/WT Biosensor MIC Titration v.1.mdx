# Background

## Objective

<Callout icon="🗣" color="green_bg">
	What is the MIC for WT EPI300 for our bacteria?
</Callout>

## Variables

Independent:  Antibiotic Concentration
Dependent: OD

# Background

To obtain better estimates for the MIC titration of our biosensors, we will perform a benchtop titration using WT EPI300.

## Procedure

### Day 1  - Prep Cultures + AB stocks + Media & Water

<span underline="true">**Prepare LB Media**</span>

1. Prep 500mL LB-Miller according to this procedure [mention-page](https://app.notion.com/p/286d65dd82be806e93a1c1e23dd23f6e)

<Callout icon="🥼" color="orange_bg">
	\~300mL prepped and autoclaved and placed on shelf F
</Callout>

<span underline="true">**Prepare Sterile Water**</span>

1. Fill 500mL bottle with MilliQ water, loosely cap and place autoclave tape
2. Run with LB Media in autoclave on LIQUID20 [mention-page](https://app.notion.com/p/1fcd65dd82be806b9959f3336e7bafb0)

<Callout icon="🥼" color="orange_bg">
	\~300mL prepped and autoclaved and placed on shelf F
</Callout>

<span underline="true">**WT Culture Prep**</span>

1. Add 5mL LB media to a culture tube, recap
2. Prepare an inoculation loop
3. Obtain glycerol stocks from -80C (where?)
4. Sterilize inoculation loop, when cooled, scrap top of glycerol stock with inoculation loop, place in culture tube and stir. Recap loosely
5. Place glycerol tube back into -80C (where?)
6. Place culture tube in 37C room, 200-250rpm, over night

<Callout icon="🥼" color="orange_bg">
	10mL prepped and place on lower shaker on 37C room
</Callout>

<span underline="true">**Prepare Gentamicin and Tobramycin Stocks**</span>

1. Read potency on antibiotics. Calculate amount needed to create a 1 mg/mL master stock of 15 mL
2. Dissolve in water, vortex, and filter
3. Aliquot 3mL into 5mL tubes, label with date and identity
4. Store in -20C

<Callout icon="🥼" color="orange_bg">
	For [mention-page](https\://app.notion.com/p/3b8d65dd82be80088e47c76f6e28cdbf) the target weight for 1mg/mL in 15mL is\:
	$15 mL \cdot \frac{1mg}{1mL} \cdot \frac{1mg}{0.6mg} = 25mg$
	Actual weight was 27.0mg → 16.2mg active, 16.2mL MilliQ H2O added
	For [mention-page](https\://app.notion.com/p/3b8d65dd82be80f8b34aff8fac006796) the target weight for 1mg/mL in 15mL is\:
	$15 mL \cdot \frac{1mg}{1mL} \cdot \frac{1mg}{0.972mg} = 15.42mg$
	Actual\: 16.4mg → 15.94mg active, 15.94mL MilliQ H2O added
	All stocks aliquoted into three tubes \~5mL, one stored in 4C others in Midea -20C
</Callout>

<span underline="true">**Working Stock Preparation**</span>

1. Into Eppendorf tubes, prepare the highest concentration of the antibiotics according to the following dilutions.
   1. These are 2x concentrations

| Tobramycin  | 32             | 32              | 968         |
| Amoxicillin | 64             | 64              | 936         |

`M` Water Moat         `G` Growth Control         `S` Sterility Control.         `1-9` Antibiotic

- The water moat is to create an evaporation buffer
- Growth control is no antibiotic to compare against
- Sterility control is the media blank

1. For each moat well, dispense 200uL sterile water
2. Dispense 100uL LB into all interior wells except column 2 (cols 3-11, rows B-G)
3. Dispense an additional 100uL LB to the sterility well (G11)
4. Dispense 200uL of the working stock into column 2
5. Mix throughly (4-5 up-downs), then pipette 100uL into the next column to the right
6. Repeat step 4 until you reach column 10, discard 100uL from column 10
   1. You should have 100uL in each well, with 2x the concentration of anbiotic
7. Repeat steps 4-6 for all rows
8. When you are done with a plate, remember to place the lid back on

<span underline="true">**Inoculum Preparation**</span>

1. Obtain the overnight culture and mix throughly
2. In a 1cm cuvette, add 100uL of the culture and 1900 uL of LB and read OD 600
   1. Use plain LB media as a blank
3. Adjust the suspension with LB to a target OD600 of 0.1
4. In a 15mL tube, add 190uL of the OD=0.1 suspension and 14.81 mL LB to create an approximately 1 \* 10^6 CFU/mL suspension
5. Invert the tube to mix
