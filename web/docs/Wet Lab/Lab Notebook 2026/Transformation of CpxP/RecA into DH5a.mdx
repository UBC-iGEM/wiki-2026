# <a id="388d65dd82be807a865dfef2f4abf3aa" />Purpose

<a id="388d65dd82be80c18e01ccd2eff9c990" />Transform the cloned plasmids (recA and CpxP) into high efficiency competent DH5a and plate.

# <a id="388d65dd82be80299df2d02c0ae17487" />Materials & Methods

<a id="388d65dd82be80a0b014e1f98f51b4f3" /><u>Materials</u>

- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80c79627cc3e6062e4dc" />Chloramphenicol LB agar plate (17 mg/L Cm concentration)
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80ae8a88dd525e9b3f3a" />NEB DH5a high-efficiency chemically competent *E. coli*
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80ff87c8ff1ce1c23437" />Cloned plasmids (recA and CpxP) from [Assembly of CpxP and RecA](/ubc-vancouver/wet-lab/lab-notebook-2026/assembly-of-cpxp-and-reca)
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80f996b2d271b7ba8602" />NEB SOC Outgrowth Medium
- <a id="3a0d65dd82be80129ccde1859ee9ff73" />42°C water bath

<a id="388d65dd82be80728adadd4b3f8f63dc" /><u>Methods</u>

1. <a id="388d65dd82be809c8c1fe4ae8fbe0b26" />Mix 9 µL of chemically competent DH5a bacteria with 1 µL of cloned plasmid (recA or CpxP)
   1. <a id="388d65dd82be80509d8ef46824194428" />pipette up and down to mix
2. <a id="388d65dd82be80a2af9bf1127344805f" />Incubate on ice for 30 minutes
3. <a id="388d65dd82be80959c04c4cafbc7a564" />Heat shock at 42°C for 30 seconds
4. <a id="388d65dd82be80d4831ee3414c179872" />Incubate on ice for 5 minutes
5. <a id="388d65dd82be800dbc6fd660cf0c60cc" />Add 100 µL of SOC outgrowth medium and incubate at 37°C for 1 hour
6. <a id="388d65dd82be805f89cfdc40e2a5f603" />Plate 50 uL on each plate and incubate at 37°C overnight
