# <a id="3ecd65dd82be801394e4efdece3683de" />SWOT Analysis

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80ab9441db7d111a9b34" />Insert SWOT analysis ASSET

<a id="3ecd65dd82be8007beedff95935b05a7" />Strength, Weakness, Opportunity and Threat (SWOT) Analysis of FAST

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80e2a276eda8916f900e" />Inherent in the product’s goal and mechanism, FAST’s strengths lies in its decentralized workflow as a portable device producing rapid results and modular design of our system for potential practical application to different infections. By reducing unnecessary downstream treatments, FAST contributes to antibiotics stewardship by supporting effective infection containment and reduces costs associated with additional treatments.

<a id="3f2d65dd82be8018bfc9fabe118fb1af" />In our current product design, FAST has limitations in the ease-of-use for direct patient administration as an at-home kit. Furthermore, as our product was focused on UTIs, the current product only supports select antiobiotic classes, though future area of research may expand to accomodate multiple antiobiotic mechanisms.

<br id="3f2d65dd82be8088a562f6366f1fd2b7" />

<a id="3ecd65dd82be809fa16bcd6ace50ac2d" />Opportunity:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80dd87f8f0d5473eaf4d" />Rapid expansion in global infectious disease healthcare concerns and greater consumer market for developing and developed countries
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80d1a81ad72cdb5490a2" />Application to multiple bacterial infections (UTI, STI etc.)
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be802a870df44ac8f5121c" />Future integration of results with personal EMRs directly

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80b39a83cee3058b31cf" />Threats:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80668a01e3cf51b2a234" />Novelty indicators
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80eb8e5dc0284ac19677" />Regulatory compliance challenges (biohazard)
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be803782f4ce97b52c4724" />Device sensitivity and error rates
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80f48e93cbb8df56b364" />Clinical validation requirements (acceptance of specific POC device results for clinical care)

  <a id="3f2d65dd82be80deb815d356ad83cca0" />Marginal - An action that will present some damage to our project, but is manageable

# <a id="3ecd65dd82be808e862ecfd58aa859b3" />Revenue Streams

<a id="3f2d65dd82be80c682b4e06d1fd14ef3" />Revenue Stream ASSET

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be80c584ecf397c4faad9e" />**Beachhead Market:**

<a id="3f2d65dd82be80d6be3df57db97a8548" />Our initial revenue will come from partnerships with local public healthcare providers and diagnostic labs. This early adoption will also serve as additional validation for our devices clinical performance.  Partnerships, specifically within UTI therapeutics, could also lead to direct sales with independent consumers. seeking the same rapid antibiotic susceptibility testing.

<a id="3f2d65dd82be800b88aae4b4c8da2e1c" />**Revenue sources here include**

- <a id="3f2d65dd82be809eafc8ea856148de78" />Potential licensing fees
- <a id="3f2d65dd82be80fd8ab9c71d8b5dd7ef" />Provincial partnership for implementation in hospitals
- <a id="3f2d65dd82be80968bf1ea7e508b38d2" />Direct sales to independent clinical diagnostic facilities

### <a id="3f2d65dd82be80a29f39c2c0a5f0009c" />**Long-Term Market:**

<a id="3f2d65dd82be80c89731f5b8dcfd2a86" />Looking ahead, our product will focus on becoming widely adopted as a reliable, rapid method of antibiotic susceptibility testing. Similar to the initial revenue, our longterm market seeks to grow and expand on partnerships but at a higher level.

<a id="3f2d65dd82be8094a4f9c8ae9b7d709d" />**We plan to generate revenue through**

- <a id="3f2d65dd82be803b886cff4be441a6ab" />Partnerships with public healthcare systems
- <a id="3f2d65dd82be80608dbef15c1d52e5cc" />Government contracts
- <a id="3f2d65dd82be80b6b91fe81e357e0ec7" />Expansion into regional and international healthcare markets
