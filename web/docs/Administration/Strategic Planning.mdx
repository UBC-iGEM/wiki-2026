# SWOT Analysis

Graphic
Strength, Weakness, Opportunity and Threat (SWOT) Analysis of FAST
Strength
Strength:

- Decentralized workflow: portable device with rapid results
- Contribution to antibiotic stewardship: reduce unnecessary treatments and support effective infection containment
- Low costs: downstream treatment costs, centralized laboratory costs
- Modular design
- Reduced necessary sample pre-treatment? —> not yet feasible

Weakness:

- Lack of ease-of-use for direct patient administration (self-administered testing kit)
- Limitations in select antibiotic classes (mechanism)

Opportunity:

- Rapid expansion in global infectious disease healthcare concerns and greater consumer market for developing and developed countries
- Application to multiple bacterial infections (UTI, STI etc.)
- Future integration of results with personal EMRs directly

Threats:

- Novelty indicators
- Regulatory compliance challenges (biohazard)
- Device sensitivity and error rates
- Clinical validation requirements (acceptance of specific POC device results for clinical care)
