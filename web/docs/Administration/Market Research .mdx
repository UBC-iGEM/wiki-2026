# <a id="3ecd65dd82be80599055f642055fdd16" />Beachhead Market

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80ce8b21dfafbeae573e" />Now that we have ascertained the potential market out there, we need a place to start. A beachhead market is a narrow, well-defined first group of customers that we can serve well before expanding. Starting with a small group of customers allows us to validate *FAST* in real settings while earning early revenue, credibility, and building client connections.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8061ba09d1747a152257" />For *FAST*, we propose an initial beachhead market of community primary care clinics in British Columbia (family physician clinics, urgent care clinics, and community pharmacies) for the detection of resistant UTI infections. We chose this group for three main reasons:

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be8000a078c2451bc02def" />**A high volume problem with little testing:** As mentioned in our Problem Statement, UTIs are among the most common bacterial infections, and because of the length of conventional susceptibility testing, most patients are started on antibiotics before results are available.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be800bbbb4d34c61b60c4c" />**An underserved prescriber:** Since June 2023, pharmacists in BC have been able to prescribe for uncomplicated UTIs, but have been met by physician pushback due to the lack of lab testing available to pharmacists <Cite refs={[{"n":5,"key":"healthPharmacistsCanPrescribe2023"},{"n":6,"key":"emailPharmacistsNowPrescribing2023"}]} />. *FAST* would help to alleviate these concerns and increase prescriber confidence in their chosen course of action.
- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80a58de5cb4daa98b15b" />**Lower costs:** Analyzer-based tests can achieve the rapidity needed for fast decisions (see our Competitor Analysis below), but require large upfront capital investments which only dedicated clinics and hospitals can justify. Pharmacies and small clinics stand to benefit from a low-cost testing method.

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80fca878dbc30c276157" />Success in this beachhead market would provide us with early revenue and the clinical validation needed to pursue expansion.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8014832ae845373969c4" />Across the competitive landscape, existing solutions fall into three groups: conventional lab testing is cheap but slow, automated lab systems are faster but still require a microbiology lab, and rapid point-of-care tests are fast but rely on expensive dedicated instruments. *FAST* aims to fill the gap between them: susceptibility information within the patient’s first treatment window, at a cost cheaper than conventional testing, without needing a dedicated lab or an expensive proprietary analyzer.

# <a id="3ecd65dd82be802cad4ad58f5437dc1d" />USP Determination

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8005bfdcf34b6ea3f027" />Based on our competitor analysis, we believe *FAST’s* unique selling point to be the ability to offer antibiotic susceptibility testing at the point-of-care setting, without requiring a dedicated microbiology lab or expensive equipment. *FAST* is a cost-effective alternative to current AST methods as it is as fast as competing rapid tests while keeping costs low. Its compact and modular design makes antibiotic susceptibility testing less labour-intensive and effective across different infectious bacteria. Its efficacy combined with its low cost and labour intensity makes FAST a desirable alternative for healthcare providers.

# <a id="3ecd65dd82be8048b376d1ec6dee8ed0" />Regulatory Environment

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80c3a692dd1246671838" />As we plan to release our product on the market, we have developed FAST with both domestic and international regulatory considerations to allow us to sell our product in multiple jurisdictions around the world. As we are using a **biosensor** in our test cassette, we need to ensure we follow biosafety guidelines, especially regulations around genetically modified organisms. As a medical device, FAST needs to follow the guidelines outlined by Health Canada for approved use in diagnostic settings. As we are using the product with potential AMR candidates, we also considered the Canadian Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance System and the Human Pathogens and Toxins Act to control the spread of pathogens that are resistant to antibiotics. Since the cassette is also handled in a laboratory setting, FAST was designed with the Canadian Biosafety Standard in mind to ensure safe handling and disposal of patient samples and potential AMR candidates.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80709f06dd34f3cd65c8" />As we strive for international release of our product, FAST was designed with the core values of the World Health Organization’s (WHO) Antimicrobial Resistance Action Plan. The Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety and the International Organization for Standardization’s guidance on medical devices were also used to help guide our product across different regulatory frameworks across the world to ensure global product compliance.

## <a id="3ecd65dd82be807fa4a9f0dde76a6d8d" />International Regulations

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be8090a96cf9f7714fb8cf" />Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety

<a id="3ecd65dd82be802d8362cfc175f67f63" />The Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety is an international agreement that governs the safe handling, transport, and use of living modified organisms (LMOs). If FAST utilizes genetically engineered bacterial biosensors, containment measures must be implemented to prevent accidental environmental release. Although FAST is intended for use in controlled healthcare settings, the protocol highlights the importance of biosafety practices when working with engineered microorganisms. It is important to note that this protocol is added specifically for releasing FAST internationally, as we plan to sell FAST across multiple markets across the world, and this protocol will generally help us abide by most biosafety protocols in every market.

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80a8bf74d74d39eed701" />Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety
  - <a id="3ecd65dd82be80ad9e63cf3a90f21444" />An international treaty that governs the handling, transport, and release of living modified organisms (LMOs), particularly those released into the environment.
  - <a id="3ecd65dd82be8048816ac2c85be277df" />Since we are handling potentially antibiotic-resistant bacteria in the testing cassette, we need to be careful not to release antibiotic-resistant bacteria into the environment

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be805ebb3be9796552c00c" />World Health Organization (WHO) Antimicrobial Resistance Action Plan

<a id="3ecd65dd82be808a87e0d9ca60c16981" />The WHO identifies antimicrobial resistance (AMR) as one of the most significant global public health threats. Through the Global Action Plan on Antimicrobial Resistance, member states are encouraged to improve antimicrobial stewardship, strengthen surveillance systems, and support the development of rapid diagnostic technologies that reduce inappropriate antibiotic prescribing.

<a id="3ecd65dd82be8055b074e3a6d053f991" />As a rapid antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) platform, FAST aligns with international efforts to improve antibiotic stewardship by enabling healthcare providers to select effective treatments earlier and reduce unnecessary antibiotic use.

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be8078a1f2c6798198ebd0" />International Organization for Standardization (ISO)

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80faa494d920fc9014b8" />Medical diagnostic devices are commonly developed under internationally recognized quality standards such as:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8005ae7fc8c055132508" />ISO 13485 (Medical Device Quality Management Systems)
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be804b99a5c21c641ba42d" />ISO 14971 (Medical Device Risk Management)
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8099ab7ac0cf142b9e3f" />ISO 23640 and related standards for in vitro diagnostic (IVD) performance evaluation

<a id="3ecd65dd82be809ca694f4e4e676434e" />Compliance with these standards supports regulatory approval and market adoption across multiple jurisdictions.

## <a id="3ecd65dd82be80aaa2a7d94ea5558f48" />Canadian Regulations

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be80faab3bdcb67f227714" />Food and Drugs Act and Medical Device Regulations

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80219fe1f8e6531eb754" />FAST would likely be regulated by Health Canada as an in vitro diagnostic medical device (IVD) because it analyzes patient samples to guide treatment decisions. Before commercialization, the device would require regulatory approval demonstrating safety, effectiveness, and analytical performance. Depending on the final intended use and risk classification, FAST may require clinical validation studies and a Medical Device Licence.

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be80929898e4090164a9d7" />Human Pathogens and Toxins Act (HPTA)

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80f08919f6e3575c977d" />Because FAST handles clinical bacterial samples that may contain antibiotic-resistant pathogens, development and testing activities must comply with the Human Pathogens and Toxins Act. This legislation regulates the possession, handling, storage, and disposal of human pathogens to reduce biosafety risks.

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be8056a00ccb03bffcf0c7" />Medical Device Regulations under the Food and Drugs Act

<a id="3ecd65dd82be80a496b5e8b3aa64e82d" />FAST would likely be regulated by Health Canada as an **In Vitro Diagnostic Medical Device (IVD)** because it analyzes patient samples and provides information used to guide clinical treatment decisions.

<a id="3ecd65dd82be800b925af58f22c3e4c1" />As an IVD, the device would require:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8075a159d825739b4a5d" />Device classification by Health Canada
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8024b6b4c0cf2b8abe15" />Evidence of safety and effectiveness
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8086bcb9c63f55c19fb4" />Quality management system documentation
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80cebc2fed310fa63953" />Clinical performance validation
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8094b835e555ba9bf02d" />Medical Device Licence prior to commercialization

<a id="3ecd65dd82be8071af54d54a48a8bcdd" />Because diagnostic results directly influence antibiotic prescribing decisions, the device would likely fall within a moderate-risk classification (likely Class II or III depending on final intended use).

### <a id="3ecd65dd82be80179e80d44c18a99547" />Canadian Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance System (CARSS)

<a id="3ecd65dd82be808a9c78f85a3046210a" />The Government of Canada actively supports initiatives that improve antimicrobial stewardship through programs such as the Canadian Antimicrobial Resistance Surveillance System (CARSS).

<a id="3ecd65dd82be806b88d0d612c9a40c84" />Rapid AST technologies align with national AMR strategies by:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8059a5efc1916cebc3f0" />Reducing inappropriate antibiotic prescriptions
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80e78a0ec91e02f8517b" />Supporting evidence-based treatment decisions
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80a28da1e9c64e9c28e6" />Improving surveillance of resistant pathogens

Specifically, we are working with Risk Group 2 organisms that cause urinary tract infections, including:

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8089b740cc8b83fc62b1" />*Escherichia coli*
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be8065b60fe2e2df28a564" />*Klebsiella pneumoniae*
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be800aada3d00e004c6656" />*Enterococcus faecalis*

- <a id="3ecd65dd82be807aa402c736286d6c74" />Appropriate containment facilities
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be80528c60e77c189c809c" />Personnel biosafety training
- <a id="3ecd65dd82be800c9041d605268d502b" />Decontamination procedures
