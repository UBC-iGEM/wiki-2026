# <a id="3fcd65dd82be80afb02ecf242a9db481" />What is antibiotic susceptibility?

<a id="3fcd65dd82be8085834cd3ef8c7a5979" />Antibiotic susceptibility determines whether a biological organism, mainly cells, can be killed by antibiotics. Through evolution, various species of pathogenic bacteria have acquired antimicrobial resistance (AMR), rendering them insusceptible to certain antibiotics. As they continue to evolve, we must control the use of antibiotics and opportunities for resistance by improving susceptibility testing and throughput.
