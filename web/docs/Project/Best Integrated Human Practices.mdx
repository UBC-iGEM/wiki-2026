# <a id="45308bca27214b5d9e005a62702c5bce" />Integration Throughout the Season

<a id="7c8374d656a84919aa2f2fca3d897b9c" />Our Human Practices work was not a single phase. It began with a structured [Investigation 1 - Literature Review](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/investigation-1---literature-review) that established the clinical and scientific picture, and which also identified the gap it could not fill: what living with resistance is actually like. We turned to [Investigation 2 - integrated Human Practices (iHP)](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/investigation-2---integrated-human-practices-ihp) to close that gap, speaking with nine clinicians, researchers, and patient partners.

<a id="7e66faf8586b49e5835e10b9e1b1ca7c" />The conversations then fed back into the work at every level. Dr. Hancock's input reached the wet lab bench by determining which antibiotics our biosensors needed to detect. Dr. Mróz's input reached our governance by determining how we were permitted to engage patients at all. Sunny Loo's input reached our front-end code by determining font sizes and animation speeds. Our [The Dialogue - What are the Barriers?](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/the-dialogue---what-are-the-barriers) page records how we moved from what we heard to the two barriers we chose to act on, and our [Our Journey](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/our-journey) page records how our own thinking changed along the way.

<a id="2c8e874c6f934123946c4800f54de37d" />The website in particular went through three documented review cycles before reaching patients broadly, with Dr. Mróz reviewing twice and Sunny Loo reviewing once, each cycle recorded as a Design-Build-Test-Learn iteration on our [Barrier 1 - Companionship Support](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/barrier-1---companionship-support) page.

# <a id="8f858cc6373f43ceaa63527916c51052" />Responsible Practice

<a id="364612ec8a51452aa68a05c10d1f8307" />We took the view that a project asking patients to share difficult experiences carries obligations beyond producing good deliverables.

<a id="d5450fbadcbc49eebba161c87946a95e" />Our engagement with patients was governed by an informed consent form, reviewed and signed by both Sunny and Norma before their interviews, which stated the topics in advance and made clear that no medical or physical information would be collected. Both were told they could skip any question or withdraw at any point. Norma's visibility on our Instagram campaign was deliberately limited to protect her privacy. The consent form is available on our [Investigation 2 - integrated Human Practices (iHP)](/ubc-vancouver/human-practices/investigation-2---integrated-human-practices-ihp) page for other teams to adapt.

<a id="bcfd273ba1d14c06935bda27263dcb92" />The same principle governed the website. Privacy was specified before any code was written, as a set of hard constraints rather than a later addition: no personal health data collected anywhere on the site, no tracking or third-party embeds, no accounts required, the visit-prep document held entirely in the user's browser, and comments never auto-published. Nothing reached patients broadly until Dr. Mróz had reviewed it twice and a patient had reviewed it once.

<a id="39a9ccd2cc0f4a16876c17378899b245" />We are also clear about what we have not established. The website remains in active iteration with Norma and has not yet been released to patient networks. Our informational support work is still in progress. We have not measured whether any of our educational materials change understanding or behaviour, and we have documented our outreach as activity undertaken rather than as impact demonstrated.

# <a id="c3274354a63243ebbf6b5f5680b81bbf" />What Other Teams Can Reuse

<a id="0ffedf2ce4c44763a94c92d9dad5efba" />The patient consent form and the framing that produced it are the most portable part of this work. Any iGEM team without ethics approval that wants to engage patients faces the same constraint we did, and Dr. Mróz's distinction between patient engagement and qualitative research is what made our approach defensible. We have documented that distinction, the consent form, and the limits we placed on ourselves.

<a id="f379023814234eb0af5826a7a929fe40" />Our barrier methodology is also reusable. Rather than building a general awareness resource, we scoped every section of our website to one evidenced need traced back to one named voice, and recorded that mapping as a table. A team can apply the same structure to a different condition and a different set of stakeholders.
