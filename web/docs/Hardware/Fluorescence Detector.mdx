# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8075b6b2f8a74de1c8fd" />Principles of Fluorescence

<a id="3f9d65dd82be8050867be53a9a577c19" />Fluorescence is critical to many experiments and research, such as flow cytometry, fluorescence immunoassay, and more. The glow helps us identify cell-structures, quantify colonies, and even acts as a signal for cell susceptibility to antibiotics.

<a id="3fad65dd82be80059b4bf8b57504d9bd" />Cells fluoresce when a fluorophore absorbs light energy (a photon), causing its electrons to reach excited state (s1) from ground state (s0). As some of the energy is lost during the process, the emitted photon contains lower energy, meaning the fluoresced light is of a longer wavelength as shown in figure 1. This phenomenon is known as the Stokes shift. \[1] [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4711767/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4711767/) This indicates that, if blue light is absorbed, green light of lower energy will be emitted by the cell, inducing fluorescence.

# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8054b0a7da2a0caffc89" />Motivation for Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3f9d65dd82be8073989bff903243bc0c" />Our microfluidic chips utilize fluorescence to binarily output information on antibiotic resistance, allowing for a quick preliminary screening before further assessments are assigned. However, we realized that the detection of fluorescence still require equipment that is likely difficult to access at the hospital, which counteracts our goal of point-of-care rapid detection. To address the problem, we further iterated and brainstormed, and eventually decided to create a complimentary fluorescence detector.

## <a id="3f9d65dd82be80b4bb54d4d68e2b0c45" />Conventional vs. Our Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3fad65dd82be8060b66ec7bc55f0ed97" />To determine the limitations of current fluorescence detectors, we conducted an initial market research. Based on the information we gathered, the price of a fluorescence detector ranges from hundreds (i.e. \$630.39 [https://www.amazon.ca/Ultraviolet-Analyzer-Fluorescence-Wavelength-Observation/dp/B0G2YH4WD5](https://www.amazon.ca/Ultraviolet-Analyzer-Fluorescence-Wavelength-Observation/dp/B0G2YH4WD5)) to tens of thousands (i.e. \$42191 [https://www.labnics.com/fluorescence-spectrophotometer/nfs-100?srsltid=AU7gw4U\_AwaKeXBQpkJ9ssq\_yOg4ckLq\_kfEVxtzd-Au4IueHhTj49xR-Dg](https://www.labnics.com/fluorescence-spectrophotometer/nfs-100?srsltid=AU7gw4U_AwaKeXBQpkJ9ssq_yOg4ckLq_kfEVxtzd-Au4IueHhTj49xR-Dg)). It is inaccessible and budget-wise unreasonable to require the equipment of fluorescence detector in each doctor’s office for only preliminary screenings. Additionally, while both hand-held and benchtop detectors are found in the market, both designs are incompatible with our microfluidics, and some may have trouble detecting fluorescence from the little amount of samples present.

<a id="3f9d65dd82be80e1b5f4d658e543124a" />To increase the accessibility of our point-of-care detection, the following adjustments are made: a holder tailored to our microfluidic chip design, convenient user interface, simplified fluorescence detection circuit to minimize the material cost, and a novel component assembly method to ensure both dark enclosure and ease of use.

<a id="3f9d65dd82be80ed8deed7516467b47d" />With our customized fluorescence detector, we make antibiotic resistance detection easier, quicker, and cheaper than ever.
