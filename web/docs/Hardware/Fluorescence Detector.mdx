# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8075b6b2f8a74de1c8fd" />Principles of Fluorescence

<a id="3f9d65dd82be8050867be53a9a577c19" />Fluorescence is critical to many experiments and research applications, such as flow cytometry, fluorescence immunoassays, and more. Fluorescence helps us identify cellular structures, quantify colonies, and even detect cell susceptibility to antibiotics.

<a id="3fad65dd82be80059b4bf8b57504d9bd" />Cells fluoresce when a fluorophore absorbs light energy (a photon), causing its electrons to transition from the ground state (s0) to an excited state (s1). As some energy is lost during this process, the emitted photon carries less energy and therefore has a longer wavelength, as shown in Figure 1. This phenomenon is known as the Stokes shift. \[1] [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4711767/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4711767/) This indicates that when blue light is absorbed, green light of lower energy may be emitted, producing fluorescence.

# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8054b0a7da2a0caffc89" />Motivation for Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80ef9515d6d95b2206a7" />Our microfluidic chips primarily utilize fluorescence to provide binary information on antibiotic resistance, enabling rapid preliminary screening before further assessments are conducted. However, we realized that detecting fluorescence still requires equipment that may be difficult to access in hospital settings, contradicting our goal of rapid, point-of-care detection. To address this limitation, we further refined our design through iterative development and brainstorming, eventually deciding to create a complementary fluorescence detector.

## <a id="3f9d65dd82be80b4bb54d4d68e2b0c45" />Conventional vs. Our Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3fad65dd82be8060b66ec7bc55f0ed97" />To identify the limitations of existing fluorescence detectors, we conducted preliminary market research. Based on our findings, fluorescence detection equipment can range in price from hundreds of dollars (i.e. \$630.39 [https://www.amazon.ca/Ultraviolet-Analyzer-Fluorescence-Wavelength-Observation/dp/B0G2YH4WD5](https://www.amazon.ca/Ultraviolet-Analyzer-Fluorescence-Wavelength-Observation/dp/B0G2YH4WD5)) to tens of thousands of dollars (i.e. \$42191 [https://www.labnics.com/fluorescence-spectrophotometer/nfs-100?srsltid=AU7gw4U\_AwaKeXBQpkJ9ssq\_yOg4ckLq\_kfEVxtzd-Au4IueHhTj49xR-Dg](https://www.labnics.com/fluorescence-spectrophotometer/nfs-100?srsltid=AU7gw4U_AwaKeXBQpkJ9ssq_yOg4ckLq_kfEVxtzd-Au4IueHhTj49xR-Dg)). Such costs make it financially impractical to equip every doctor’s office with a fluorescence detector used only for preliminary screening. Furthermore, although both handheld and benchtop detectors are available on the market, their designs are not compatible with our microfluidic chips. Some may also struggle to detect fluorescence from the small sample volumes used in our system.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80868e51fbc00a29dc5b" />To improve the accessibility of our point-of-care detection system, we incorporated several design features into our customized fluorescence detector: a holder tailored to our microfluidic chip, a user-friendly interface, a simplified fluorescence detection circuit to reduce material costs, and an innovative component assembly method that ensures both effective light shielding and ease of use.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80519f6edb341fb10bad" />With our customized fluorescence detector, we aim to make antibiotic resistance screening more accessible, more rapid, and more affordable.

<a id="3fbd65dd82be80b4baddccfa2fa0bdd6" />Before beginning our journey in designing a fluorescence detector, we researched on some previous iGEM works to see what has been achieved in the past.

<a id="3fbd65dd82be80a2b6c4d594c87938f4" />Team Munich’s design in 2017 inspired us: they came up with a compact fluorescence detector design by integrating magnets to sandwich detection components in between 3D printed parts \[4]. We realized the potential of magnets in enhancing user-friendliness and introducing a convenient method of assembly.

<a id="3fbd65dd82be80fcbf3dd64607f5505b" />As demonstrated in the 3D model (demonstrate in 3d model), the detector is split into two halves: A and B (label). Part A acts as the bottom, encompassing a sliding track for the microfluidic holder and circuit components that detect fluorescence. Part B will go on top of Part A to create an all dark enclosure; it also contains the Arduino microcontroller, illumination, and user interface for active control and feedback.

## <a id="3fbd65dd82be8055acb5c9d629e47f52" />Improved Detection Workflow

<a id="3fbd65dd82be80988d13d51f15f40090" />We studied the mechanism of fluorescence detection used in conventional detectors, and simplified them so they get the same task done at a way cheaper cost.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80b1a2f2eb0a0ccfdead" />Most fluorescence detectors adopt a similar layout, with the illumination component, the sample, and the detection component forming a 90 degrees angle. This arrangement helps improve signal quality by reducing background noises \[3]. Excitation filters and emission filters permit a narrow band of wavelength, such that only the desired lights are filtered through and shone on the sample or detector \[5 [https://www.thorlabs.com/filters-for-fluorescence-microscopy-tutorial?tabName=Tutorial](https://www.thorlabs.com/filters-for-fluorescence-microscopy-tutorial?tabName=Tutorial)].

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80bea79cf9c45374b2e6" />While this set-up has been widely approved, a critical limitation is the high cost of the filters. Since both filters need to allow a small range of light through, the precision determined their high price. After our research, we found that one excitation filter costs around \$300.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be80199d18ceb6e7dfc17b" />To address this issue, we identified the key purpose of each component, and reengineered the conventional detection workflow to a more succinct and cost-effective form. Leaving the most essential parts, our detector encompasses a single-color LED directly above the sample, and a longpass filter and photodetector directly below.

<a id="3fcd65dd82be803a80ffc5a7513e587a" />The function of each component is explained in detail in Design Breakdown. Overall, this layout reduced the material cost by ?? % (need to double check with Saige), saves a lot more space, and provides better accommodation for the microfluidics design.

#### <a id="3fcd65dd82be8016a5bddf3afc0838d6" />**We plan to continue exploring the potential of our customized fluorescence detector. The following are some of our next steps:**

- <a id="3fcd65dd82be80c48737ec42f030366e" />**Implement a fully automated detection process**
  - <a id="3fcd65dd82be80b399c0d57eec43d687" />Adopt a rack-and-pinion mechanism, with the rack attached to the bottom of the chip holder and the pinion positioned directly beneath it.   - <a id="3fcd65dd82be801dbddaed9cc5815f6c" />Automatically upload all detection results (fluorescence/no fluorescence) to the connected computer, allowing users to attend to other tasks and retrieve the complete results at any time.
