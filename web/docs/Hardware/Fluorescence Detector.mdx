# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8075b6b2f8a74de1c8fd" />Principles of Fluorescence

# <a id="3f9d65dd82be8054b0a7da2a0caffc89" />Motivation for Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3f9d65dd82be8073989bff903243bc0c" />Our microfluidic chips utilizes fluorescence to binarily output information on antibiotic resistance, allowing for a quick pre-liminary screening before further assessments are assigned. However, we realized that the detection of fluorescence still require equipment that is likely difficult to access at the hospital, which counteracts our goal of point-of-care rapid detection. To address the problem, we further iterated and brainstormed, and eventually decided to create a complimentary fluorescence detector.

## <a id="3f9d65dd82be80b4bb54d4d68e2b0c45" />Conventional vs. Our Fluorescence Detector

<a id="3f9d65dd82be80208b7dd28ffef5be09" />Fluorescence detectors may vary in sizes and looks, but they can be quite expensive due to the materials used, which is unfriendly for budget if merely using for rough screenings. Additionally, due to the design of our microfluidic chip, most fluorescence detectors found on the market are not compatible.

<a id="3f9d65dd82be80e1b5f4d658e543124a" />To increase the accessibility of our point-of-care detection, the following adjustments are made: a holder tailored to our microfluidic chip design, friendly user interface, simplified fluorescence detection circuit to minimize the material cost, and a novel component assembly method to ensure both dark enclosure and ease of use.

<a id="3f9d65dd82be80ed8deed7516467b47d" />Overall, our fluorescence detector are improved in cost, usability, efficiency, and compatibility.
