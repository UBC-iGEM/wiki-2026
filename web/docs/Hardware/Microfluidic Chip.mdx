# Introduction and Overview

## What is a microfluidic chip?

Microfluidic chips are an emerging technology within the biomedical space. They function by using a series of grooves engraved into a wide variety of materials, including but not limited to PDMS and acrylic ([@pattanayakMicrofluidicChipsRecent2021]). The channels within the microfluidic chip are specifically designed to bring about the creator’s desired result. Microfluidic chips assist in microscale mixing, diagnostics, and small-scale chemical reactions ([@pattanayakMicrofluidicChipsRecent2021]).
