## <a id="3ecd65dd82be806182b5e89574979a70" />What is a microfluidic chip?

<br id="3edd65dd82be80a6a3ffe70a149035d0" />

<a id="3ecd65dd82be8021b470f88b3f0261b1" />Microfluidic chips are an emerging technology within the biomedical space. They function by using a series of grooves engraved into a wide variety of materials, including but not limited to PDMS and acrylic (\[@pattanayakMicrofluidicChipsRecent2021]). The channels within the microfluidic chip are specifically designed to bring about the creator’s desired result. Microfluidic chips assist in microscale mixing, diagnostics, and small-scale chemical reactions (\[@pattanayakMicrofluidicChipsRecent2021]).
