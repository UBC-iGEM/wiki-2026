title: "Impact of the Bioreactor"
---

The completed bioreactor demonstrates that a small-scale system built from relatively simple and accessible components can support *E. coli* growth comparable to conventional benchtop equipment.

Growth curves recorded from validation were compared against those produced from standard laboratory methods, giving a practical way to evaluate whether this simplified design could match, or better yet, exceed the expected bacterial growth behaviour. Beyond its engineering success within UBC iGEM 2026’s project scope, the nature of an open-source design marks it as a starting point for smaller teaching laboratories and research groups, or even medical centers in resource-limited environments that want to adapt and expand on this point-of-care device. The main objective of developing the bioreactor is not to replace high-end laboratory equipment, but to showcase that many functions required for bacterial culture can be recreated on a much smaller scale and at a lower cost.
