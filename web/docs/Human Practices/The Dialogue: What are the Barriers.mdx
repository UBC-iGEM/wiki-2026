<a id="3d8d65dd82be80b1abfdd25b44ddd09b" />There is a serious gap between how AMR is understood medically and how it is experienced by patients. Insight from the physicians confirm literature findings that the burden of AMR extends far beyond treatment failure. Patients may experience uncertainty, isolation, stigma, repeated screening, communication gaps, and barriers created by prolonged or resource-intensive treatment. These burdens can be amplified by healthcare-system constraints and socioeconomic circumstances. Addressing AMR requires not only better antimicrobials and stewardship, but also accessible education, patient-centred communication, and more emphasis on empathy and psychosocial support.
