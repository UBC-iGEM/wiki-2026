Abstract
We sought to identify high quality promoters—characterized by dynamic range, low basal expression, and low cross-activation—that enable quantifying presence of antibiotic in solution, essential to the wet lab development of our project. To characterize over 200 promoter candidates, we developed the experimental design of a high-throughput experiment executed by Biofactorial, an automation facility.
