# UBC iGEM 2026 sponsor logo pack

The `sponsors/` directory contains seven original logo assets for the confirmed sponsors. The source files were downloaded from official sponsor websites or the official UBC Brand & Marketing unit-signature directory. The logo artwork was not recreated, recoloured, cropped, or otherwise edited. PNGs retain their original transparency and proportions.

## Use notes

- UBC Brand's current logo page says the unit and department packages are available to UBC faculty and staff, and directs users to contact Brand and Marketing if a unit is missing. The linked official directory still contains the MBIM, SBME, and MSL packages, but their package names and Dropbox modification dates identify them as 2016/2017-era releases; UBC does not show a newer revision in that directory. Verify with the sponsor/team lead that these marks are acceptable for this 2026 sponsor display before publishing.
- The team has confirmed permission to use the UBC marks and SnapGene logo in this sponsor display. UBC unit signatures must remain unaltered and keep their clear space and minimum-size requirements. Review the [UBC Brand visual identity rules](https://assets.brand.ubc.ca/downloads/ubc_brand_identity_rules.pdf) and [SnapGene terms](https://www.snapgene.com/legal-disclaimers) when reusing these assets.
- Keep every logo's aspect ratio. Do not recolour, crop, or apply effects.
