#### Best Sustainable Development Impact

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a call to action on global
environmental, social, and economic challenges, and this award is your team's
way to answer that call. Demonstrate how you have evaluated your project ideas
against one or more SDGs, consulted SDG stakeholders, and started to build
collaborations with other iGEM teams around the goals. You are encouraged to
revisit previous iGEM projects, evaluate them against the SDGs, and build on
them.

Visit
the [Special Awards page](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/special) for
more information.
